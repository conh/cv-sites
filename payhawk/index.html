Treasury isn't a separate product bolted onto your account. It's the same platform you already use for cards, expenses and multi-entity management, now able to put your excess cash to work too.

How it works

  1. Turn it on.

    Enable Treasury for your eligible entity, the same way you switch on any other Payhawk module.

  2. Choose an amount.

    Move as much of your excess cash into Treasury as you want, directly from your existing balance.

  3. It earns.

    Safeguarded with J.P. Morgan and invested in a money market fund, at one flat, disclosed rate.

  4. Move it back anytime.

    Same day, no notice period, no lock-in.

Have a question?

Which entities are eligible today?

Your UK entity, if you're on a multi-entity plan. Other regions join as we clear FCA/EEA licensing for each.

What happens to my other entities?

Nothing changes for them yet, they'll get the same access as their region clears.

How do I move money in and out?

From your Treasury module, move any amount from your operating balance in, and move it back the same day, whenever you need it.

How fast can I get funds back?

Same day.

Is this like a bank deposit?

No. Treasury is a money market fund, not a deposit account, so it isn't FSCS-covered the same way.

What happens to the fee after early access?

Nothing hidden, it's the same flat, disclosed margin stated above, the waiver just ends.
